# Community code of conduct｜社群守則

[English README](README.md) · [繁中首頁](README.zh-TW.md) · [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) · [貢獻方式](CONTRIBUTING.zh-TW.md)

## Welcome｜歡迎參與

Beginners, experienced facilitators, developers, and translators are welcome. English and Traditional Chinese are both welcome; clear questions and small corrections are useful contributions. Critique an idea or behavior, not a person's identity or worth.

歡迎新手、主持人、開發者與翻譯者。中英文都可；清楚的提問、小修正也有價值。討論想法或行為，不攻擊人的身分與價值。

## Expected behavior｜共同期待

- Be respectful and specific. Explain disagreements and allow room for corrections.／尊重且具體地表達不同意見，保留修正空間。
- Use public or synthetic examples. Protect participants, clients, and other contributors.／使用公開或虛構案例，保護參與者、客戶與貢獻者。
- Separate fictional personas from actual people, sourced facts, and endorsements.／區分虛構角色、真人、已查證事實與背書。
- Report evidence honestly: do not fabricate transcripts, test results, quotations, or adoption claims.／如實呈現證據，不捏造逐字稿、測試結果、引言或採用成績。
- Respect attribution, third-party rights, and the project's pending license decision.／尊重來源、第三方權利及全庫授權待決狀態。

## Unacceptable behavior｜不接受的行為

Harassment, threats, hate or discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private information, impersonation, spam, and persistent personal attacks are not welcome. A fictional council or provocative starter-group name is not an excuse for harmful conduct.

不接受騷擾、威脅、仇恨或歧視辱罵、性騷擾、肉搜、蓄意公開隱私、冒名、洗版與持續人身攻擊。虛構角色或挑釁式組名不是傷害他人的藉口。

## Reporting and moderation｜通報與處理

For ordinary non-sensitive conduct concerns, flag the relevant public discussion for maintainer review without escalating the conflict. Do not repost harmful private material or identify affected people unnecessarily.

一般、不涉敏感資料的行為問題，可標示相關公開討論請維護者檢視，不升高衝突，也不要重貼傷害性私人內容或不必要地揭露受影響者。

For sensitive reports, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)／[安全政策](SECURITY.zh-TW.md). Use a private reporting channel only if one is actually available. Otherwise ask the maintainer how to report privately, without including the sensitive details. No private inbox or guaranteed response time is promised here. If no safe channel is available, do not post the report publicly.

敏感事件依安全政策處理。有實際可用的私人管道才使用；否則先詢問維護者如何私下通報，不附敏感細節。本守則不宣稱已有私人信箱，也不保證回應時效。沒有安全管道時，不要改成公開貼出事件內容。

Maintainers may request edits, hide or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation according to severity and repetition. Where appropriate, they should explain the rule involved without exposing private details. These expectations apply to project Issues, pull requests, and other project-managed community spaces, including maintainers themselves.

維護者可依嚴重程度與重複情況要求修改、隱藏或移除內容、關閉討論或限制參與。適當時說明涉及規則，但不洩露私人細節。規則適用於本專案 Issue、PR 及其他由專案管理的社群空間，也適用於維護者。
