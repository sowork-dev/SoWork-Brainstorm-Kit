## Low-effort controls | 懶人指令

- 繼續 ↔ Continue
- 換組／換人 ↔ Swap the cast / Replace a member
- 更大膽 ↔ Go bolder
- 更務實 ↔ Make it practical
- 你決定 ↔ You decide — choose and develop a direction, not automatically reset the cast.／選擇並展開方向，不自動重設人物組合。
- 教我練習動腦 ↔ Teach me to practice brainstorming — explicit TEACHING opt-in.／明確選用真人練習。
