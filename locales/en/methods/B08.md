## B08 | Brainwriting (also called Slip Writing)

**When to use / Suggested time**: Everyone needs a chance to express their own ideas independently first; 15–25 minutes.

**Opening**: “No talking this round. Let each sheet bring an idea into view.”

**Process**: Everyone writes silently → pass sheets in the same direction → read and add ideas that build on them → complete the agreed number of passes → collect the results before discussing and selecting ideas.

**Follow-up / Output**: “Which sheet sparked a new connection, rather than just another way of saying the same thing?” Produce a collection of relay-developed ideas. Use A02 if you adopt the fixed setup of six people, three entries, and five minutes.[3]
