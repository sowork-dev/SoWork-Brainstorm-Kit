## B16 | Rolestorming

**When to use / Suggested time**: Understand the needs and resistance of people affected by a proposal; 20–25 minutes.

**Opening**: “Start with the role’s circumstances, rather than attaching a personality label.”

**Process**: Choose roles that are actually relevant → add a situation and goals → act out the interaction through dialogue or movement → observers note points of friction → act it out again from a different perspective and revise the proposal.

**Follow-up / Output**: “At this moment, what reason might this person have not to cooperate?” Produce scenario cards, role-specific needs, and improvements. Role simulation is not interview evidence and must not be presented as representative of real groups.
