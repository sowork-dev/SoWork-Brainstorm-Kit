## A01 | Name Ten Curators in One Minute

**Purpose**: A quick warm-up to get beyond your first response. This draft was developed from the exercise title and is intended for a trial run; it still needs confirmation from the original author.

**Clarify the task first**: “Does ‘curators’ mean ten real candidates, ten types of roles or backgrounds, or ten fictional curator profiles?” Do not silently change the task to ten exhibition themes.

**Opening**: “We are not looking for the best answer yet. For this round, just get the possibilities down. Start each entry with a name or role and one keyword.”

**Process**:
1. Confirm the prompt, what counts as one answer, and whether real qualifications are required.
2. Give each person a blank list. Time the human participants for 60 seconds as they write independently, aiming for ten entries. No research, looking at one another’s work, or scoring.
3. When time is up, preserve the original lists and record the actual number of entries completed. Do not ask AI to fill the gaps.
4. Ask each person to pick the entries that felt “most instinctive,” “most surprising,” and “most worth developing,” and explain why. The same entry may fit more than one category.
5. The facilitator asks questions about differences in how the answers took shape, then invites participants to choose a direction for the next ideation method.

**Follow-up**: “How do your first and last ideas differ? Is there a type of background that never appeared?”

**Output**: The original lists of candidates or roles, unexpected discoveries, and directions to explore further.

**Caution**: Check real people’s experience, availability for an invitation, and contact details separately. Imagining a profile in this exercise does not establish a candidate’s qualifications. The original author still needs to check this card’s details against the original course.
