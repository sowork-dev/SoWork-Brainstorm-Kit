## B17 | Reverse Brainstorming

**When to use / Suggested time**: People keep giving stock answers, or you need to identify how failure could happen; 15–20 minutes.

**Opening**: “Suppose we deliberately wanted to make this worse. What would we do?”

**Process**: Reframe the goal as a failure question → collect actions that would cause failure → group them by failure mechanism → propose prevention measures or opposite designs for each mechanism → check them against the original positive goal.

**Follow-up / Output**: “Which of these bad practices are already happening?” Produce failure mechanisms and countermeasures. Keep the discussion focused on identifying risks; do not turn harmful ideas into implementation instructions.[3]
