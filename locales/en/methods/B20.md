## B20 | Rapid Ideation

**When to use / Suggested time**: Expand the pool of candidate ideas first; allow a few minutes for ideation, plus separate time to organize the results.

**Opening**: “Write quickly this round. Do not stop to explain why each idea must be feasible.”

**Process**: Agree on the prompt and time limit → write independently without stopping → share the original drafts after time is up → categorize without erasing differences → let participants choose surprising but promising directions.

**Follow-up / Output**: “If you could not use that type of answer again, what would your next entry be?” Produce the original candidate list. A01 can serve as a warm-up with a specific prompt for this method. Repeated ideas are not proof of effectiveness.[3]
