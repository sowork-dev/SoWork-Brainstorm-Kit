## B04 | How Now Wow

**When to use / Suggested time**: You have a collection of ideas and need to sort them into next steps; 10–15 minutes.

**Opening**: “Use originality and current feasibility to decide where each idea should go next.”

**Process**: Present ideas one by one → position them by originality and feasibility → sort them into Now (familiar and feasible), Wow (original and feasible), and How (original, but still needing a way to make it work) → clarify disagreements about placement first → decide what to try now and what needs further research.

**Follow-up / Output**: “What is missing from the How ideas that would make them feasible?” Produce a sorting table. Do not automatically rank an original idea as the best proposal when its feasibility remains unproven.
