## B27 | Storyboarding

**When to use / Suggested time**: Examine how someone experiences a proposal step by step; 20–30 minutes.

**Opening**: “Do not just say it will be moving. Draw how the person gets to that moment.”

**Process**: Choose one user and situation → arrange key moments from before, during, and after the experience → write the actions and touchpoints in each panel → identify breakdowns and information gaps → revise and walk through it again.

**Follow-up / Output**: “How does the person know what to do next? What if they stop at this panel?” Produce a sequence of storyboard panels for discussion. Use as many panels as the situation calls for; drawing skills are not required.
