## B06 | Mind Mapping

**When to use / Suggested time**: Explore different aspects of a topic; 15–20 minutes.

**Opening**: “Let the map grow outward first. Do not rush to turn it into a proposal outline.”

**Process**: Put the topic in the center → each participant suggests first-level branches → add concrete examples to each branch → mark connections across branches → choose a fresh path worth developing.

**Follow-up / Output**: “What kinds of people or situations are still entirely missing from our map?” Produce a branching map and directions for further exploration. AI organizes the human participants’ branches first, then labels its own additions separately.[3]
