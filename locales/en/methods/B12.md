## B12 | Lightning Decision Jam

**When to use / Suggested time**: Move from frustrations to action; this draft suggests 45–60 minutes.

**Opening**: “Let everyone write independently first, then use clear steps to make choices instead of going around in circles in discussion.”

**Process**: Write down what is going well and what is getting in the way → human participants vote on the priority problem → reframe it as ‘How might we…?’ → independently generate solutions → human participants vote and compare impact against effort → specify one small experiment and a review point.

**Follow-up / Output**: “We do not need to solve everything. What is the smallest thing we can try this week?” Produce a priority problem, candidate solutions, and an experiment card. This card is a practical summary. See the introduction provided by the method authors for LDJ’s authorship and principles; this card does not claim to reproduce their full official teaching materials.[6]
