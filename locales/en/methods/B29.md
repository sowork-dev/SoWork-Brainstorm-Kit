## B29 | Crazy Eights

**When to use / Suggested time**: Quickly explore a range of forms; eight minutes for the core exercise, plus time for sharing and selection.

**Opening**: “Make eight different attempts in eight panels. They do not need to look polished, and they should not all be decorative variations of the same sketch.”

**Process**: Prepare eight panels → sketch eight concepts in eight minutes → aim to explore a different mechanism or form in each panel → choose a few panels to share after finishing → participants decide which panel is worth developing further.[5]

**Follow-up / Output**: “If this panel could not rely on visual effects to attract attention, what interaction mechanism would remain?” Produce eight-panel sketches and priority concepts. Without a drawing interface, use short text in eight panels instead, but label it a text adaptation.
