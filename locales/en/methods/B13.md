## B13 | Idea Napkin

**When to use / Suggested time**: Turn a vague idea into a proposal other people can understand; 15–20 minutes.

**Opening**: “Use one sheet for one concept. Do not let a catchy name stand in for substance.”

**Process**: Each person chooses one idea → fill in the audience, situation, problem, approach, and expected value → explain with short sentences or sketches → others ask clarifying questions only → compare proposals worth trying.

**Follow-up / Output**: “Without naming the activity, describe what happens on site. What would people see?” Produce one-page concept cards that can be compared.
