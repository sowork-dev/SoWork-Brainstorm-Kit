## B28 | Wishing

**When to use / Suggested time**: Bring out hopes that constraints have kept people from expressing; 15–20 minutes.

**Opening**: “Start with ‘I wish…’ and resist adding ‘but’ straight away.”

**Process**: Each person makes a wish → explain the need behind it → separate the real goal from the exaggerated form it takes → identify obstacles and resources to draw on → propose the smallest version that preserves the core value.

**Follow-up / Output**: “If you could realize only the most important small part of the wish, which part would that be?” Produce needs, an ideal state, and the smallest proposal worth trying.
