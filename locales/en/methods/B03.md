## B03 | SWOT Analysis

**When to use / Suggested time**: Compare proposals in light of resources and the wider environment; 20–25 minutes.

**Opening**: “First distinguish the internal conditions we can control from changes in the external environment.”

**Process**: Agree on the subject of the analysis → individually list internal strengths, internal weaknesses, external opportunities, and external threats → give reasons for these assessments → identify combinations to take advantage of or guard against → turn them into specific strategic hypotheses.

**Follow-up / Output**: “Is this a strength backed by evidence, or something we hope is true about ourselves?” Produce the four quadrants and priority strategies, not just a list of adjectives.
