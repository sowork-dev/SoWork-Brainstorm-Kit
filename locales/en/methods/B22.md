## B22 | Stepladder Brainstorming

**When to use / Suggested time**: Preserve independent thinking, then bring it into the group step by step; 25–40 minutes.

**Opening**: “New arrivals share their own ideas before hearing the existing answers.”

**Process**: Everyone understands the prompt and prepares independently → two people stay to discuss first → invite a third person to join and share first → discuss together → add the remaining people one at a time, then organize the results.

**Follow-up / Output**: “Which earlier assumption does this new perspective change?” Produce a record of the joining order, independent perspectives, and how the ideas evolved. Online sessions need actual breakout rooms or another way to separate those who have not yet joined. Do not pretend an ordinary group chat can hide earlier messages.
