## B01 | Starbursting

**When to use / Suggested time**: You have a candidate concept and need to identify what is still unclear; 15–20 minutes.

**Opening**: “Do not rush to defend the proposal. Ask about what we do not yet know.”

**Process**: Choose one concept → ask who, what, when, where, why, and how questions → collect questions first, without rushing to answer → separate questions into what is known and what needs research → decide which questions to clarify first.

**Follow-up / Output**: “Which question, if answered incorrectly, could undermine the entire proposal?” Produce a prioritized question list and assign fact-checking responsibilities. Miro also supports this question framework.[3]
