## B25 | Change of Scenery

**When to use / Suggested time**: Discussion has stalled and needs fresh observations; 10–20 minutes.

**Opening**: “Step away from the way we have been discussing this. Take the same question with you and observe.”

**Process**: Keep the prompt fixed → change location, take a walk, or look at different material → each person records one new observation → return and explain its connection to the prompt → develop changes that can be tested.

**Follow-up / Output**: “Which newly noticed detail helped you move beyond the original solution?” Produce observations and new candidate ideas. If the change of scenery is only imagined in chat, label it an imagined version; do not claim human participants actually moved.
