## B15 | Figure Storming

**When to use / Suggested time**: Use different figures’ ways of thinking to break habitual patterns; 15–20 minutes.

**Opening**: “Borrow a figure’s traits without pretending we know what that person would actually say.”

**Process**: Choose an international figure or fictional character as a lens → state the traits you are borrowing → ask how that way of thinking would approach the prompt → record new approaches → return to real-world conditions and select what could transfer.

**Follow-up / Output**: “Which thinking principle are you borrowing, beyond the person’s fame?” Produce perspectives and candidate approaches. Label the content as participants’ imaginings, not as a real person’s endorsement or quotation.
