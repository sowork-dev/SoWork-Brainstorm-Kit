## B05 | Drivers Analysis

**When to use / Suggested time**: Understand how several factors influence the same outcome; 15–20 minutes.

**Opening**: “Rather than looking for just one cause, lay out the factors that drive the outcome.”

**Process**: Agree on the outcome to improve → list possible drivers → identify underlying factors for each driver → mark the degree of control and the evidence → choose one factor worth testing first.

**Follow-up / Output**: “Would changing this factor alone really change the outcome? What counterexamples are there?” Produce a factor tree and questions to test. Unlike the earlier card’s single-path questioning, this card deliberately keeps multiple branches.
