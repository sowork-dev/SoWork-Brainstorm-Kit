## B10 | Brain-netting (also called Online Brainstorming)

**When to use / Suggested time**: A distributed team needs to keep collecting ideas online; agree on a collection period first.

**Opening**: “Put ideas in one shared place so good ones do not get scattered across private messages.”

**Process**: Specify an actual shared submission space → standardize the submission fields → participants contribute and respond whenever they wish → regularly categorize contributions and flag items needing clarification → bring everyone together at the deadline to compare and decide.

**Follow-up / Output**: “Which threads are actually addressing the same issue?” Produce an online idea bank and a decision record. An ordinary AI conversation does not automatically become a shared, real-time space for multiple people.
