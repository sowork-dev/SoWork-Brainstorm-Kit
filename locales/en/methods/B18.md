## B18 | Reverse Thinking

**When to use / Suggested time**: Compare your usual practices with how others might approach the task; 15–20 minutes.

**Naming note**: This card follows Asana’s description of ‘how others would approach it,’ keeping it separate from the deliberate-failure approach in B17. It does not claim that all literature uses this name in the same way.

**Opening**: “If a completely different team took this on, which step would they change first?”

**Process**: Write down the current approach → specify another working style or team context → propose alternative solutions → compare the conditions that would make them effective → decide which parts can be borrowed, need adjustment, or do not apply.

**Follow-up / Output**: “Do we have the conditions that make it work elsewhere?” Produce a comparison of usual practices and alternative strategies. Do not invent other teams’ actual practices.
