## B24 | What-if Brainstorming

**When to use / Suggested time**: Explore new paths when conditions change; 15–20 minutes.

**Opening**: “Change one condition at a time and see whether the original answers still hold up.”

**Process**: Write down current assumptions → pose a what-if condition → find approaches that work under that condition → switch to another condition → return to reality and choose mechanisms that could transfer.

**Follow-up / Output**: “If there were no venue, the budget were cut, or the audience were completely different, which part would need to change first?” Produce a condition–approach comparison table. Clearly distinguish hypothetical scenarios from actual conditions.
