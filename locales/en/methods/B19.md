## B19 | Eidetic Imagery

**When to use / Suggested time**: Explore an activity, product, or service experience; 10–15 minutes.

**Opening**: “Imagine a specific moment of use. If you prefer not to close your eyes, look at a blank sheet of paper.”

**Process**: Describe the current setting → quietly imagine how someone moves, looks, listens, and acts in that setting → draw or write the scene → imagine a scene after improvements → compare the two and suggest design changes.

**Follow-up / Output**: “At what moment might the person stop, feel confused, or want to continue?” Produce experience scenes and candidate improvements. Do not treat imagined scenes as user-research facts.
