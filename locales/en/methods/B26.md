## B26 | Random Word Association

**When to use / Suggested time**: Break out of a loop of similar answers; 10–15 minutes.

**Opening**: “Accept this outside word for now. Do not immediately dismiss it as unrelated to the prompt.”

**Process**: Confirm the prompt → a human participant draws a word or specifies an outside word → list its properties and mechanisms → connect one of these to the prompt → choose useful connections and make them concrete.

**Follow-up / Output**: “What if you borrowed how this thing works, rather than what it looks like?” Produce a word–association–proposal chain. A word chosen offhand by AI should be called a prompt word. Do not claim random sampling was performed unless a sampling tool was used.
