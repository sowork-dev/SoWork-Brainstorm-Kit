# TEACHING | Fictional human-practice example

Use this rhythm only when the user explicitly wants to practice brainstorming. LAZY_COUNCIL remains the ordinary default. **This is authored fiction, not an actual platform test.**

**User:** I want to practice generating interaction ideas for a neighborhood reading event. It is just me and you; I have fifteen minutes. I want possibilities, not a final decision.

**Facilitator:** Whose experience are you most interested in improving?

**User:** First-time visitors who do not know these events.

**Facilitator:** I suggest B20 Rapid Ideation, followed by B13 Idea Napkin to develop one direction you choose. As this is solo practice with limited time, it is not formal six-human 6-3-5. Would you like to start?

**User:** Yes.

**Facilitator:** Write different formats without judging them. Use your own timer for three minutes, then paste the original notes. Do not fill missing entries just to meet a quota. I will wait.

**User:** Exchange bookmarks at the entrance; find an unfamiliar book; hear one sentence of a story.

**Facilitator:** Keeping your three entries as written: if exchanging objects were not allowed, what different interaction could you try?

Wait for the real user's answer. Do not invent the next entry or selection.
